Privacy Policy

THE SAFE SPACE’S DISCLAIMER ON PROCESSING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

Because Safe Space platforms handle sensitive personal information, transparency, security, and user trust are even more important than usual. The cookie documentation is structured to meet privacy regulations (POPIA, GDPR, and ePrivacy Directive) and to ensure that the platform is compliant, ethical, and user-friendly.

The Safe Space platform is committed to protecting the privacy, dignity, and safety of all individuals who use our services.

1. Purpose of the Website/System

Safe Space platform is intended to provide information, resources, and support related to sexual abuse, teenage pregnancy, weapons, bullying, substance abuse, and violence. It may include reporting tools, referral services, educational material, and connections to support organizations.

We use cookies to ensure our website functions securely and effectively for users seeking support or reporting gender-based violence (GBV). These cookies help us:

  1. Enable essential website functionality (e.g., secure login, form submissions).
  2. Support user safety (e.g., anonymous sessions, auto-logout for safety).
  3. Improve user experience (e.g., remembering language preferences).
  4. Gather non-identifiable analytics to improve accessibility and services.

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings or our consent options.

By accessing SAFE SPACE’s information system, you acknowledge that you have read and accepted this disclaimer on processing of Personal Information.

For the purpose of this disclaimer, “Personal Information” and “Data Subject(s)” are as defined in the Promotion of Access to Information Act (PAIA), the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), and Electronic Communications and Transactions Act.

2. Purpose of Personal Information Processing

Personal information provided (including names, contact details, demographic information, and case-related data) is collected solely for the purpose of providing support, reporting, case management, referrals, and related services to survivors and stakeholders in GBV matters.

Information may also be processed for research, awareness, and statistical reporting, but in these cases, all identifying details will be removed (anonymized).

3. No Emergency Services

This platform is not an emergency response service. If you are in immediate danger or need urgent assistance, please call your local emergency number (e.g., 10111 in South Africa) or contact nearby law enforcement, healthcare, or support services immediately.

Your information will be treated with strict confidentiality and only accessed by authorized personnel who require it to perform their duties.
